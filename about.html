Our story

Marketing that
means something

Lochside Creative is a social media and creative agency built for ambitious service-based businesses who want more: more bookings, more confidence, more visibility, and more freedom.

We help you grow your business with content, branding and strategy that actually works — so you can stop guessing, stop blending in, and finally show up online as the leader you are.

We built Lochside Creative for business owners who want to grow with intention, not burnout. You didn't start your business to spend your evenings posting on Instagram, second-guessing every caption, or trying to DIY your brand identity. You started it for freedom. To do what you love. To make an impact. To build something meaningful. And that's exactly where we come in.

Our mission is simple: to help service-based businesses leverage social media and branding to fuel real growth — more enquiries, more confidence, and a brand presence you're genuinely proud of.

Co-founder of Lochside Creative

Meet
Molly

A marketing expert with years of hands-on social media experience. She first launched Socials by Mol in May 2024 after feeling completely trapped in the 9–5 and knowing deep down she was meant to build something of her own. She's wanted a business for as long as she can remember.

As a Gen Z business owner, Molly grew up with social media. She started micro-influencing at 16, which is where she discovered her passion for creating content that actually connects. Since then, she's worked with a huge range of service-based businesses — from dentists and decorators to law firms, online coaches, and hair salons — helping them build a strong presence online.

Her mission is to help service-based business owners leverage social media to fuel real, sustainable growth. She specialises in content marketing and built her entire first business completely organically without ads, just strategy. She helps brands attract their ideal clients, increase bookings, elevate their online presence, and position themselves as industry leaders.

Since launching Socials by Mol, she quickly outgrew the brand (retaining clients all year and hitting max capacity alone), which led her to evolve the business and co-found Lochside Creative alongside her partner, Owen.

Together, they're building something bigger.

Co-founder of Lochside Creative

Meet
Owen

Owen started Lochside Creative with Molly because they wanted freedom — the freedom to work on their own terms, build something meaningful, and use their creativity to help other people create that same freedom in their own businesses.

His background isn't the typical design route. He's always been creative, always drawing, and when he served in the Royal Marines, sketching tattoos and artwork became one of his outlets. Whether it was drawing, tattoo design, or skateboarding, creativity was something he always came back to.

Over time, that passion evolved into design and eventually into branding. He realised he could combine creativity with strategy to make a real impact for businesses. He studied graphic design for 18 months, and that's where he truly found what he loves.

Now, at Lochside Creative, he helps businesses transform how they're seen. Good branding doesn't just look good, it builds trust, positions you as the expert, attracts your ideal clients, and gives you the confidence to value your work properly.

His role is simple: take businesses that are blending in, and create a brand that stands out, feels authentic, and grows with them.

For him, it's never just about creating a "pretty logo." It's about building brands that position you as the go-to in your industry, attract the right clients effortlessly, and give you the space to focus on running your business while your branding does the heavy lifting.
